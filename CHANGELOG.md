# Changelog

## 1.0 - First testing release

The first testing build of PodHopper for iOS and Apple Watch.

PodHopper is an independent, privacy-first fork of [Pocket Casts](https://github.com/Automattic/pocket-casts-ios), the podcast app open-sourced by Automattic. This initial release removes the inherited ads, analytics, and third-party tracking, rebrands the app as PodHopper, and moves account and sync onto Cover Two Strategies' own infrastructure.

- Privacy first: no ads, no analytics, no third-party tracking.
- PodHopper account with cross-device sync of your subscriptions, queue, and playback position.
- Apple Watch app with pairing-code sign-in and standalone playback.
- Sync Account to Car App: sign a car into your account with a code entered on your phone.
- Account management from the app: create, sign in, sign out, and delete your account.

This is an early testing release and is under active development.
